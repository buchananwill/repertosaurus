# Vision Index

Where the product should arrive and why, in the product's own language, before any spec narrows
it. A vision carries no ordering; specs and journals do.

- [From Logger to Nudger — the Habit Former](from-logger-to-nudger.md) (`active`): moving
  from recording practice to supporting a long-term routine. The app assists and never imposes. It covers suggest a song, timed practice, habit
  scorecards, triage by priority and confidence per song–performer–role, and a visual identity
  with user-chosen colour ramps. The one-tap log stays sacred throughout.
